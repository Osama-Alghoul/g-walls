Frequently Asked Questions

What is ICF (Insulated Concrete Forms)?

Insulated Concrete Forms (ICF) are stay-in-place formwork systems made from expanded polystyrene (EPS) insulation. The forms are assembled on site and filled with reinforced concrete to create strong, energy-efficient, and durable wall systems.

Why choose ICF over traditional construction methods?

Compared to conventional wood or masonry construction, ICF offers:

  • Higher structural strength
  • Superior thermal insulation
  • Improved fire and disaster resistance
  • Faster construction timelines
  • Enhanced acoustic performance
  • Reduced material waste

What is the R-value of iForm® ICF walls?

The iForm® ICF wall system achieves an R-value of up to R-22, based on the complete wall assembly, including EPS insulation, concrete core, and interior finish.

Is ICF construction more expensive than traditional methods?

While initial materials costs may be higher, ICF construction typically delivers long-term savings through reduced energy consumption, lower maintenance, and faster construction, which can reduce overall project costs.

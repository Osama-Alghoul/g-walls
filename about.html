About us

G-Walls is a provider of insulated concrete form (ICF) systems and EPS-based building solutions in the Middle East and North Africa region. Our systems are designed to meet the structural, thermal, and regulatory requirements of contemporary construction across commercial, residential, and industrial building sectors.

Our Story
& Values

Gulf Walls System was established to introduce advanced construction technologies to the Saudi market. As pioneers of Insulated Concrete Form (ICF) manufacturing in the Kingdom, GWALLS combines international expertise with local manufacturing to deliver building systems aligned with global standards and regional requirements. Our approach is driven by innovation, engineering discipline, and a long-term commitment to sustainability and performance.

Why Insulated Concrete Forms (ICF)

ICF technology delivers measurable benefits across structural performance, energy efficiency, and construction logistics.

Energy Efficiency

Continuous insulation reduces thermal bridging and improves building envelope performance, leading to lower operational cooling and heating loads.

Structural Strength

Reinforced concrete core provides superior compressive strength and resistance to lateral loads compared to conventional framing methods.

Acoustics & Comfort

Mass and insulation combination delivers excellent sound attenuation, creating quieter interior environments in noise-sensitive applications.

Construction Speed

Integrated formwork eliminates separate insulation installation, streamlining the construction sequence and reducing trade coordination.

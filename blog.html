iForm® Insulated Concrete Forms (ICF)

Project Overview

iForm® is an advanced insulated concrete form (ICF) system engineered to deliver superior structural strength, energy efficiency, and long-term durability for residential, commercial, and industrial construction.

As construction standards evolve, the demand for high-performance wall systems has increased significantly. iForm® Insulated Concrete Forms respond to this demand by combining reinforced concrete and continuous insulation into a single, integrated building system.

iForm® panels consist of two fire-retardant EPS insulation layers connected by high-strength polypropylene ties. Once filled with concrete, the system forms a monolithic wall that delivers exceptional thermal, acoustic, and structural performance.

System Overview

iForm® functions as permanent formwork for cast-in-place reinforced concrete walls. The insulation panels remain in place after the concrete pour, providing continuous thermal insulation on both interior and exterior wall surfaces.

MATERIAL COMPOSITION

Material Composition

  • Expanded Polystyrene (EPS): Fire-retardant, lightweight insulation with excellent thermal properties.
  • Polypropylene (PP) Connectors: High-strength ties that maintain panel alignment and provide attachment points for finishes.
  • Durability: Weather-resistant exterior finishes
  • Reinforced Concrete Core: Provides load-bearing capacity and long-term structural durability.

PERFORMANCE BENEFITS


Applications

iForm® ICF systems are suitable for:

  • Villas and residential buildings
  • Commercial and retail facilities
  • Schools, hospitals, and institutional buildings
  • Industrial and utility structures
  • Basements, retaining walls, and safe rooms

Compliance & Sustainability

iForm® supports sustainable construction by reducing operational energy consumption and material waste. The system is manufactured using certified raw materials and has been independently tested in accordance with applicable ASTM standards for thermal, mechanical, and fire performance.
